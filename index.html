About Lulu

I am an ICF PCC certified Career & Leadership Coach and former senior project and team leader at Riot Games.

I work with professionals and leaders who are navigating career transitions, leadership challenges, and moments of uncertainty.

My coaching combines strategic thinking, deep listening, and practical action to help clients gain clarity, build confidence, and create meaningful change.

My clients have:

  • Stepped into leadership roles they once thought impossible
  • Got higher titles and better compensation after layoffs
  • Started businesses they had only dreamed about
  • Became leaders that people follow and respect
  • Built stronger, happier teams
  • Discovered their true calling and successfully turned it into a career

My clients consistently share how I help them shift perspectives, turning challenges into opportunities. Through our work together, they develop a more strategic mindset, communicate better, find courage to take bold steps, and approach their careers with renewed energy and purpose.
